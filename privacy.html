Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Turnless is made by Awessem Gaming. This policy explains what our game and this website store, and where it goes. In short: we don't collect your personal data.

What the game keeps on your device

Turnless saves these files on your own computer or phone. They never leave it, except through Steam Cloud if you play on Steam (see below).

  • Your save: progress, settings, decks, unlocks, your familiar, and the climber name you choose.
  • Your last replay: the cards played in your most recent fight, so you can watch it again.
  • Feedback notes: if you use the in-game feedback form, your note is saved as a file on your device and copied to your clipboard. Nothing is sent unless you choose to paste it somewhere.

We have no servers, accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and we never sell or share data with anyone.

Steam

If you play on Steam, Valve handles these features under the Steam Privacy Policy:

  • Achievements, linked to your Steam account.
  • The daily leaderboard: your Steam name, your time and basic run details (your job and deck size) are posted and visible to other players.
  • Steam Cloud: a copy of your save file, so it follows you between computers.

Google Play and itch.io

On Android and itch.io, the game itself sends nothing. Google and itch.io handle purchases and downloads under their own privacy policies.

Fair play

Your save contains a check that shows whether it was edited outside the game. An edited save still works normally; it just can't post to the leaderboard or unlock Steam achievements. This check runs on your device and is never sent anywhere.

This website

awessem.com uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from other sites. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep visitors' IP addresses in its server logs for security, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Turnless is not made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anything from them. We don't collect personal data from anyone.

Deleting your data

  • On your device: uninstall the game, or delete its save folder.
  • Steam: you can remove Steam Cloud data through Steam. To have a leaderboard entry removed, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
