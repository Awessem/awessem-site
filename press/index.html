Short description

Turnless is a real-time card battler where every second is a turn. Mana flows for both fighters at once, so you play the moment you're ready, and Counter the moment they commit.

Long description

There is no end-turn button in Turnless. Both fighters draw on mana that fills every second, and any card you can afford goes down the instant you play it. Every fight becomes a read: spend now on a big hit, or hold your mana for a Counter and turn their best card back on them.

Choose from twelve jobs across four classes, each with its own cards, a passive that changes how it plays, and a Zenith, a fight-changing power that charges up during battle. Then climb the Clocktower: pick boons that bend the rules, survive its glitches, and face the bosses at the top. Every clear opens harder Ascension levels.

There are no card packs, paid passes or crafting currencies. Every card, outfit, emote and familiar is earned by playing.

Features

  • Real-time card battles: no turns, fights of a minute or two
  • 12 jobs in 4 classes, each with its own cards, passive and Zenith
  • Counters and Reads: punish your opponent the moment they commit
  • The Clocktower: boons, glitches, bosses and Ascension levels
  • A new daily challenge every day
  • Familiars to earn, name and look after
  • Seven languages
  • Nothing to buy after the game
